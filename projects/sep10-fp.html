SEP 10 Freedom Project

I am a student at HSTAT in the Software Engineering Program. The Freedom Project for SEP10 is a year-long project where we make a website that informs viewers about current and future innovations in a topic of our choosing. For my project, I chose the topic of forensic science. I used HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, GitHub, and A-Frame to build my website. I also independently studied forensic science to gather information and help me create my site.

For this project, I created a website about forensic science and the technologies used to investigate crimes. The website includes information about existing forensic technologies, their prices, and future innovations that could improve the field, organized into different sections using a navigation bar, accordions, cards, images, and responsive layouts with Bootstrap. I used HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, GitHub, and A-Frame to build the website and create an interactive experience for users. I also did self-research on forensic science technologies to make sure the information was accurate and up to date. One part of the website includes a simulation related to quantum computing, which connects to my ideas about future forensic technology. The project required careful research and planning to make sure the information was organized and easy to understand. This project helped me improve my web development skills by giving me more experience with Bootstrap components, website organization, and combining research with coding. It also helped me communicate my ideas more effectively by presenting slideshows about my project. In the future, I will use the lessons from this project in SEP 11 when we focus on game design, and I will continue building on these skills throughout the upcoming years of computer science in junior and senior year..

This project required a clear plan because I created two wireframes before building the website: one for phones where everything stacked on top of each other, and one for computers where images, cards, and text were neatly arranged side by side. I had to manage my time carefully to make sure I finished each section of the website on schedule while building it with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, GitHub, and A-Frame. I sometimes struggled because the website would look weird on certain devices, so I had to go back, check my layout and structure, and fix the design.
